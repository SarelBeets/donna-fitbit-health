Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 23 August 2026

Purpose

Donna Fitbit Health is a personal, non-commercial connector used by its authorised user to track Fitbit Aria 2 body-fat and weight trends. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Data accessed

With the user's Google OAuth consent, the app requests only googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly. It reads body-fat, weight, timestamp, and minimal source metadata needed to identify eligible Fitbit scale measurements. The app cannot modify Google Health data.

How data is used

Data is used solely to calculate private baselines, trends, targets, and safety guardrails for the authorised user's personal health tracking. It is not used for advertising, profiling, eligibility decisions, or any unrelated purpose.

Storage, disclosure, and security

  • Normalised measurements are stored on the user's private workstation.
  • OAuth credentials are stored in the user's private 1Password vault.
  • No health measurements or credentials are stored on this public website or repository.
  • Personal data is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or disclosed to unrelated third parties.

Retention, deletion, and revocation

Local data is retained only while the user wants the connector to operate. The user can revoke access at any time in their Google Account permissions and can request deletion of the local measurements and OAuth credential through the contact address below.

Google API Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
