Donna Fitbit Health

Donna Fitbit Health is a private connector that reads an authorised user's Fitbit Aria 2 body-fat and weight measurements through the Google Health API. It calculates local trend summaries for personal health-goal tracking.

  • Access: health metrics and measurements, read-only.
  • Processing: on the user's private workstation.
  • Sharing: no sale, advertising use, or third-party disclosure.
  • Medical use: informational trend tracking only, not medical advice.
